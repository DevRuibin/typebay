Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

TypeBay is a free typing speed test that runs entirely in your browser. We built it to be private by design:

What we collect

Nothing. TypeBay has no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising. Your keystrokes, typing results and settings never leave your device.

Where your data lives

Your settings, typing history and saved results are stored in your browser's own storage (localStorage / IndexedDB) on your device only. Clearing your browser data removes them. No server ever receives them.

Third parties

TypeBay loads no third-party scripts. The service is hosted on Cloudflare's network, which handles standard network requests and may keep routine, short-lived connection logs for security and operation — the same as any website host.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page.

TypeBay is a modified fork of the open-source project Monkeytype, licensed under the GPL-3.0.
